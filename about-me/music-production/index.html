Production

Music Production

Anton's workflow blends the organic feel of acoustic tracking with advanced digital sequencing. Working in Logic Pro, he routes and processes complex MIDI data, sequences multi-layered tracks, and mixes with the ear of a trained composer and the precision of an engineer.

Approach

Playing the horn on his own sessions changes how the productions get built. Arrangements are written knowing what a real player will do with them, tracked live where a live take serves the music, and sequenced where sequencing does the job better. The mix decisions follow from the arrangement rather than being applied to it afterward.
