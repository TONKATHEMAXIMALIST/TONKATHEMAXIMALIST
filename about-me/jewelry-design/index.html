Rizvanov Custom Silver

Jewelry Design

Precision carried over from the circuit board to precious metal. Every chain is woven link by link and closed by hand, with the same attention to structural integrity, weight, and finish that the work demands anywhere else.

  • Sterling silver
  • Stainless steel
  • Bronze
  • Hand-woven weaves
  • Made to measure

Necklace Chains

Byzantine, half byzantine, box weave

Neck chains are woven from individually cut and closed rings, then finished and polished as a single piece. Weave, gauge, and metal are chosen together, because they determine each other: a heavier ring wants a longer chain to drape correctly, and a tighter weave changes how the piece catches light.

Customization: specify length to the quarter inch, ring gauge, weave, and metal. Mixed-metal runs are available, alternating sterling and steel sections within one chain. Clasp style is chosen to match the weight of the piece so it never becomes the weak point.

Wrist Chains

Bracelets in the same weaves, built to fit

A bracelet takes more abuse than a neck chain and gets looked at more closely, so wrist pieces are woven tighter and soldered where the load sits. Fit matters more here than anywhere else in the catalogue: too loose and the weave twists, too tight and it stops moving with the wrist.

Customization: sized to an actual wrist measurement rather than a standard length, with the weave and gauge matched to a neck chain if you want the set to read as one. Solid or hollow construction available depending on the weight you want on the arm.

Earrings

Bronze Age Collection and hanging chain work

The earring work runs in two directions. The Bronze Age Collection is sculptural — small blades with wrapped hilts and crossguards, each one filed and finished individually, which means no two are identical even within a pair. The hanging chain pieces apply the same weaves used in the chains at a much smaller scale, where ring gauge has to drop far enough to stay light on the ear.

Customization: matched or deliberately mismatched pairs, drop length to your preference, and hypoallergenic hooks or posts on request. Single earrings are available for anyone who wears one.

Rings

Forged, wrapped, and stacking

Rings are where the metal gets worked hardest — forged, wrapped, and shaped around a mandrel rather than cast, so the grain of the material follows the form. The pieces are built to stack, with profiles that sit against each other cleanly when several are worn on one hand.

Customization: sized to a specific finger, including knuckle-fit adjustments for larger knuckles. Bands can be finished bright, brushed, or oxidized to sink the texture, and a set can be commissioned as a coordinated stack rather than as separate pieces.
