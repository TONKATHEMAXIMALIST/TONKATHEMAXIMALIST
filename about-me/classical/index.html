Classical Composition

Drawing on deep foundational theory, Anton writes and arranges complex chamber and orchestral works. His compositions reflect a rigorous understanding of voice leading, harmony, and orchestration, bringing structured beauty to the page before it ever reaches the stage.

CALIBRATION

Three movements for solo piano with orchestral accompaniment

CALIBRATION traces a descent and what came after it. It was written out of the lowest stretch of Anton's life, and it is concerned with the mechanics of that period rather than the drama of it: how independence curdles into isolation, how failure learns to repeat itself, and how much of that repetition is chosen.

What the work finally argues is simple enough to state and hard to live: the only way forward is to recognize beauty, create it, spread it, and let yourself sit inside it. The three movements are the calibration itself — the process of adjusting an instrument that has drifted until it reads true again.

I. Autonomy

2:36

Self-determination stated plainly, before it turns into isolation. The piano leads and the orchestra concedes ground to it.

II. The Cycles of Failure

3:17

The longest stretch of the descent, built on material that keeps returning to where it started with the damage compounding each time around.

III. Acceptance

3:40

Not resolution as triumph. The material from the first two movements returns recognized rather than resisted, and the piece ends inside the beauty it spent two movements arguing toward.

Scherzo Duet in D Minor

Piano and trumpet · full score

A concert duet written for piano and trumpet in D minor, engraved as a full score. The writing puts the two instruments in genuine conversation rather than assigning one the melody and the other the accompaniment, with the scherzo character carried by displaced accents and fast handoffs between the parts.

Arranging and Reharmonization

Alongside original concert writing, Anton reharmonizes existing material as a way of testing how far a familiar melody can be moved before it stops being itself. His reharmonization of Once Upon a December is on the main page.
